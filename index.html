Song Ideas

Happy & bright

  • “Somewhere Over the Rainbow”

  • “Let It Go”, from Frozen
  • “A Million Dreams”, from The Greatest Showman
  • “Respect”, Aretha Franklin
  • “Defying Gravity”, from Wicked

Save these for after your warm-up. They build to high notes.
